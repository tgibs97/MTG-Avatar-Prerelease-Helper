Event Basics

Prerelease window: Nov 14–20, 2025.

Each Prerelease Pack contains:

  • 5 Avatar Play Boosters
  • 1 Seeded Character Booster aligned to your box’s color/character

    The only official statement about these boosters is: “Each Character Booster focuses on a specific color of Magic and includes cards that highlight your chosen character.” It’s probably safe to assume you’ll receive a version of your character’s card, so those are showcased below, as they’ll likely guide your deckbuilding at prerelease.

  • 2 non-foil double-sided tokens themed to the pack’s color
  • 1 traditional foil year-stamped rare or mythic rare
  • 1 color-themed spindown die

Note: If you want a specific character, check with your local game store about distribution.

Draft Archetypes

W/U FLYERS

"Take to the skies with Aang and his friends! Gather a team of flyers and soar like an Airbender."

U/B DRAW TWO

"Hunt for knowledge, using Clues and other draw effects to trigger your second-draw reward cards. The long game is yours!"

B/R FIRE NATION AGGRO

"Everything changes when the Fire Nation attacks! Be aggressive with firebending and burn it on extra effects."

R/G EARTH RUMBLE RAMP

"Use mana ramp and earthbending to create 4+ power creatures, then rack up the bonuses to claim the title belt."

G/W ALLIES

"A world united is unstoppable Play Allies and generate tokens to go wide and hit your enemies hard."

W/B SACRIFICE

"Victory comes at a cost. Play smaller creatures and Clues, then gain the upper hand when you sacrifice them."

U/R COMBAT LESSONS

"Play creatures that care about casting noncreature spells, then teach your foes a lesson with your martial prowess."

B/G +1/+1 COUNTERS

"Accrue power by any means! Stack up counters to gain bonuses and control the Earth Kingdom from within."

R/W GO WIDE AGGRO

"Lead the rebellion with an army of Allies and tokens that you can enhance for devastating attacks."

G/U RAMP + LESSONS

"Turn your knowledge into power! Ramp and draw cards to play big creatures while getting Lessons in your graveyard"

Archetype summaries adapted from MagicUntapped (Oct 29, 2025).
